# Logo Generation Prompt

For use with Gemini, DALL-E, Midjourney, or other image generation tools.

## Full Prompt (Catppuccin Mocha Focused)

```
Create a logo for "Vauchi" in the Catppuccin Mocha aesthetic — a cozy, soothing pastel color scheme popular in developer tools and terminal themes.

APP CONTEXT:
Vauchi is a privacy-focused contact card sharing app. Users exchange cards in person via QR code, and cards stay updated with end-to-end encryption.

CATPPUCCIN MOCHA AESTHETIC:
- Warm, cozy feeling with soft pastel accents on dark backgrounds
- NOT harsh or high-contrast — gentle, easy on the eyes
- Inspired by coffee shop warmth (Mocha = darkest, coziest variant)
- Colors should feel harmonious and soothing, never jarring

EXACT COLORS TO USE (Catppuccin Mocha palette):
Background layers:
- #1e1e2e (Base) — main dark background
- #181825 (Mantle) — slightly darker layer
- #11111b (Crust) — darkest shadows

Accent colors (choose 2-3 for the icon):
- #89b4fa (Blue) — soft sky blue, trustworthy
- #94e2d5 (Teal) — calming, connection
- #cba6f7 (Mauve) — gentle purple, privacy/security
- #a6e3a1 (Green) — soft mint, verified/success
- #74c7ec (Sapphire) — deeper blue alternative

Light elements:
- #cdd6f4 (Text) — soft white for contrast

VISUAL CONCEPT:
- Two overlapping contact cards (representing exchange)
- Small shield or lock badge (representing privacy)
- Rounded corners, soft shapes — nothing sharp or aggressive
- The "cozy" feeling should come through in soft gradients between Catppuccin colors

STYLE REQUIREMENTS:
- Flat design with subtle depth (soft shadows using Mantle/Crust colors)
- Minimal, clean — works as small app icon
- No text in the logo
- Circular or rounded square container using Base (#1e1e2e)
- Icon elements in Blue (#89b4fa) and/or Teal (#94e2d5)
- Privacy badge in Mauve (#cba6f7)

MOOD:
- Cozy like a warm coffee shop
- Trustworthy and calm
- Modern developer aesthetic
- Privacy-conscious but not paranoid

DO NOT USE:
- Any colors outside the Catppuccin Mocha palette
- Harsh whites (#ffffff) — use #cdd6f4 instead
- Pure blacks (#000000) — use #11111b instead
- High contrast or neon colors
- Complex gradients or glossy effects
- Busy details that won't scale down

OUTPUT:
- Square format (1:1)
- Vector-style clean edges
- Works at 16x16 and 512x512
```

## Short Prompt

```
Catppuccin Mocha themed logo for "Vauchi" — a privacy contact sharing app.

Aesthetic: Cozy pastel-on-dark like popular developer themes. Warm, soothing, not harsh.

Visual: Two soft rounded contact cards overlapping with a small shield badge. Minimal, flat design.

EXACT colors only:
- Background: #1e1e2e (dark blue-gray)
- Cards: #89b4fa (soft blue) and #94e2d5 (teal)
- Shield: #cba6f7 (pastel purple)
- Shadows: #181825 (darker layer)

Style: Cozy developer aesthetic, works as app icon, no text.
```
